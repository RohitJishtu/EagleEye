## 💡 Pro Tips

1. **Don't Rush**
   - Understanding > Speed
   - Take notes by hand
   - Implement every example

2. **Active Learning**
   - Draw diagrams
   - Explain concepts out loud
   - Teach someone else

3. **Practice Daily**
   - 2-3 hours minimum
   - Consistency beats intensity
   - Review previous weeks

4. **Use EagleEye**
   - Study this codebase
   - Identify patterns
   - Understand trade-offs
